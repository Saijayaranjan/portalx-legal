Support

PortalX is an unofficial app and is not affiliated with SRM.

Report a problem or ask a question: open a support request. We usually reply within two days.

Common questions

I can't sign in

Use your SRM NetID (without @srmist.edu.in) and your Academia password. Academia allows only about ten sign-ins a day, so if it refuses, wait and try again later. If a picture of characters appears, type them exactly.

My attendance or marks look out of date

Pull down on the screen to refresh. PortalX shows what Academia shows; if Academia hasn't updated, PortalX can't either.

How do I remove my data?

Sign out in Account, or delete the app. See the privacy policy for server data.
